From a field record to rate analysis

Record the project, work item, date, measured output and unit. Link the original measurement and capture material use, labour hours and equipment use for the same work. Review the quantities and unit costs before using them in the rate analysis workbench.

  1. Record: update the correct company materials, daily-work, attendance or measurement Sheet.
  2. Check: keep receipts, photos and measurements; confirm completed work separately from planned work.
  3. Update: the website refreshes after its normal publishing check. Changes may not appear immediately.
  4. Use elsewhere: reviewed records can support site progress, reminders, scheduling and costing after those connections are enabled.
  5. Keep history: retain prior records and supporting documents even after work is completed.

The drawing list and historical payments are updated through the website publisher. Materials, attendance and daily-progress workbooks are linked for entry, but their entries do not yet update other websites automatically.
